What we do

Restore the systems your business relies on.

More than editing. We address information quality across the knowledge lifecycle, including AI-generated video and audio.

01 / Remediate

Knowledge remediation

Inventory repositories, identify duplicate and obsolete assets, resolve conflicting versions with content owners, and consolidate or archive approved material.

Deliverables: Content inventory, disposition register and prioritized cleanup plan.

02 / Verify

Information integrity

Check source provenance, flag unsupported claims and fabricated references, and route technical assertions to qualified reviewers.

Deliverables: Verification register and evidence-linked corrections.

03 / Audit AI

AI output assurance

Sample AI-generated deliverables against agreed accuracy, completeness and usability criteria. Identify recurring failure patterns.

Deliverables: Evaluation rubric, error analysis and review recommendations.

04 / Structure

AI readiness

Improve taxonomy, metadata, document structure and authoritative source mapping. Test representative retrieval questions before and after changes.

Deliverables: Metadata schema, retrieval test set and readiness report.

05 / Govern

Knowledge governance

Define ownership, review cycles, publishing standards, approval paths and retention rules aligned with client policy.

Deliverables: Governance playbook and lifecycle workflow.

06 / Maintain

Continuous information hygiene

Monitor quality indicators, review new content and track remediation actions. Editorial refinement is available for priority materials.

Deliverables: Periodic quality dashboard and maintenance backlog.

07 / Refine media

AI video & audio refinement

Make synthetic media more natural, credible and useful without misleading audiences. We refine corporate training videos, AI-narrated podcasts, onboarding modules and virtual presenters.

Prompt engineering & iterative editing

Develop scene-level prompts, refine scripts and storyboards, and iterate on pacing, visual continuity, gestures, narration and audio quality. Review drafts with human stakeholders.

Synthetic media assurance

Review factual accuracy, accessibility, captions and transcripts. Assess impersonation and deepfake risks, verify likeness and voice permissions, and retain clear AI disclosures and provenance where supported.

Deliverables: Prompt library, revised media assets within agreed scope, iteration log, quality checklist and synthetic-media risk report.

Quality measures: Reviewer-rated naturalness, instructional clarity, continuity defects and factual corrections.

How we work

Audit. Restore. Maintain.

Step 01

Discover

Agree scope, permissions, success measures and data handling. Start with a bounded repository.

Step 02

Diagnose

Inventory assets, run automated screening and manually validate samples. Establish a baseline.

Step 03

Restore

Prioritize remediation, consult subject experts and obtain approval before changing or archiving content.

Step 04

Sustain

Retest retrieval and quality, hand over governance standards and offer ongoing monitoring.

Client-approved tools and access only. No irreversible deletion without authorization. Subject matter experts validate domain-specific accuracy.
